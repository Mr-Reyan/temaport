Ship Chandler Freetown

Ship Chandler Freetown provides professional ship supply, provisions, bonded stores, deck and engine stores, safety equipment, spare parts and marine procurement services.

Ship Chandler Freetown provides comprehensive ship chandling, marine procurement and vessel supply services for commercial ships calling at the local port, terminals and surrounding anchorage areas. Ship Chandler Freetown works with ship owners, ship managers, vessel operators, captains, chief engineers, purchasing departments, technical superintendents and local agents who require reliable supplies within the limited time available during a port call.

The main objective of Ship Chandler Freetown is to provide one dependable point of contact for a broad range of vessel requirements. Whether a vessel requires fresh provisions, bonded stores, deck stores, engine stores, cabin and galley items, safety equipment, electrical materials, marine chemicals, tools, spare parts or urgent technical supplies, Ship Chandler Freetown can coordinate sourcing and delivery according to the vessel's ETA, berth, anchorage position and operational schedule.

Fast communication is essential in ship supply. Ship Chandler Freetown therefore focuses on prompt RFQ handling, clear quotations, correct product identification, practical alternatives when requested items are unavailable and efficient coordination with the vessel or appointed agent. Advance requisitions are always recommended, but Ship Chandler Freetown can also assist with urgent requirements when local availability and port conditions permit.

Professional Ship Supply Services by Ship Chandler Freetown

Ship Chandler Freetown provides ship supply support for a wide range of merchant and commercial vessels. Every order is reviewed according to the vessel's actual requirements, specifications, quantities and requested delivery time. The aim is to reduce the workload of the vessel's purchasing team by combining multiple categories of stores into one coordinated supply.

Customers can send requisitions in Excel, PDF, Word or other commonly used formats. IMPA and ISSA references, maker names, model numbers, photographs, drawings and technical specifications can be included to help Ship Chandler Freetown identify the correct products.

The supply range of Ship Chandler Freetown covers routine consumables as well as urgent and non-standard requirements. Where the exact requested brand is unavailable, Ship Chandler Freetown can offer an alternative for customer approval before delivery.

Please include:

  • IMO number

  • Port of delivery
  • ETA
  • ETD if available
  • Berth or terminal if known
  • Anchorage information if applicable

  • Quantities

  • Maker and model information
  • Photographs or drawings for technical items

A complete RFQ allows Ship Chandler Freetown to respond faster and reduces the need for repeated clarification.
