About Ship Chandler Tema

SHIP CHANDLER TEMA PORT CO., LTD — professional ship chandler services at the Port of Tema and all Ghanaian commercial ports.

Your Trusted Partner in Port of Tema

Ship Chandler Tema Port

Ship Chandler Tema delivers end-to-end maritime procurement and professional vessel provisioning for ships calling at the Port of Tema terminals. We work closely alongside fleet managers, shipowners, technical superintendents, purchasing executives, ship masters, and local shipping agencies to ensure seamless, punctual onboard delivery throughout the vessel's port turnaround.

Our commitment is to function as a unified, reliable agency desk for every vessel supply need in Ghanaian waters. Whether delivering prime galley provisions, bonded goods, deck consumables, or technical engine-room spares, our dispatch desk synchronizes each order precisely with the ship's confirmed ETA, dock assignment, port timetable, and individual requisition list.

Ship Chandler Tema operates strictly as dedicated marine suppliers rather than general freight forwarding agents. Our core specialization centers exclusively on ship chandlery: fresh food provisions, galley stores, deck and engine supplies, bonded stores, crew beverages, and cabin consumables.
