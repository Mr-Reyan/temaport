Regional Maritime Support

Supporting Vessels Across West Africa

Ship Chandler Tema PORT CO., LTD operates as a unified supply desk for vessels calling at the major commercial ports across the West African coastline. From the bustling container terminals of Tema to the industrial hub of Takoradi and the strategic deep-water ports of Lomé and Cotonou, we coordinate comprehensive vessel provisioning and technical procurement.

While our core operations are anchored in Tema, our network extends to support vessels in Lagos, Abidjan, and Douala, ensuring that ship owners and managers have a reliable point of contact regardless of their port rotation in the region.

Tema

Ghana's largest port and container hub, and our headquarters.

Takoradi

Ghana's main export port for cocoa, timber, and minerals.

Lomé

Togo's deep-water port, a key transshipment hub.

Cotonou

Benin's primary port, serving regional trade.

Lagos (Apapa)

Nigeria's busiest port complex and commercial capital.

Abidjan

Côte d'Ivoire's major port, a key West African gateway.

Douala

Cameroon's largest port, serving Central African trade.
