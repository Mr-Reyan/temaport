Port of Tema

The Gateway to West Africa

The Port of Tema is Ghana's largest port and a critical maritime hub for West Africa. We ensure your vessel is fully stocked and ready for the next leg of its journey across the Atlantic and beyond. Our local team guarantees seamless coordination from quayside to anchorage.
